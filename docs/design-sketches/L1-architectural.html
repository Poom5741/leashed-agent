FIG. 1 · Four pieces

What the trust layer is made of.

Four chain-enforced promises that together turn an AI worker into something a human can actually fund, audit, and fire.

01

Leash

Spending cap set by the human, enforced by the chain itself. Revocation is instant, on-chain.

02

Receipts

A CRE workflow re-checks the whole ledger and attests the verdict on Sepolia. Neither the agent nor we can forge it.
