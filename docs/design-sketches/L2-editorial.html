§ 1

The problem

An AI agent already spends money. It asks a language model for a tagline. It hires an image model to render the poster. The payments clear on-chain, in milliseconds, with no one on the other end asking whether the spend was sensible. That is the problem. No cap that holds, no receipt that survives the call, no third party that signs off on the books.

x402 micropayments moved 75.4 million transactions last August and $24.2M in volume. The transaction count grew. The oversight didn't. We watched the lane and saw the same gap the judges will see: a payment rail without a trust layer.

"A payments primitive without an audit primitive is a primitive that can't scale to a CFO." — the platform hypothesis, 06 Oct 2026
§ 2

The four pieces

We built the four pieces the lane was missing. Not a feature. Not a product. A trust layer. Each piece is a separate job, not a checkbox on a slide deck.

Each piece is independently useful. Each piece is also load-bearing for the others — the leash makes the receipts meaningful; the receipts make the audit possible; the audit makes the funding trustworthy; the funding the leash human-settable. Take one out and the other three stop working.

§ 4

The architecture, in one paragraph

A passkey wallet on ThaiFi owns one or more leashed agents. Each agent has a P256 access key authorized on-chain with a per-token spending cap. When the agent runs a job it pays per call — ThaiFi MPP for one set of services, Cardano x402 for another — and every payment becomes a receipt. The CRE auditor fetches the receipts on a cron, runs the same risk checks as the Hono in-process route POST /api/audit, and attests the verdict on Sepolia. The owner sees the ledger at https://leashed-agent-platform.pages.dev/agents and can fire any agent with one click.

§ 5

Four tracks, four integrations, one product

Main Cardano Chainlink CRE NOWNodes

Each track submission has to earn its place — the rules disqualify bolt-ons. So we made each integration core. Cardano x402 is one of the two payment rails, not a demo. CRE is the auditor, not an afterthought. NOWNodes is the RPC data plane the auditor fetches receipts from. Main is the whole product. None of the legs are decorative.
